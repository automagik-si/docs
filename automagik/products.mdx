You can use Automagik's parts on their own. Start with the part that fits your work.

| Product | What you get | Next step |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Desktop | Bots and chats in one window. | [Install](/automagik/getting-started) |
| Bot team on DSH | Bots and group chats in DSH. | [DSH guide](/automagik/install/dsh) |
| Code mode on Herdr | Spaces, Bots and workers in Herdr. | [Guide](/automagik/install/herdr) |
| Genie | Skills and a CLI that take a task through design, code and review. | [Genie](/genie) |
| Omni | Connect chat channels and route their messages to agents. | [Omni](/omni) |
| Pocket | Your coding agents on Android. | [Android guide](/automagik/install/android) |
| usagemeter | Records token usage and cost on the computer. | [Pack](/automagik/install/herdr) |
| mikro | A CLI for agents to explore large codebases and documents. | [mikro](/mikro) |
| Arca | Be your own cloud. Infrastructure for AI agents. | [Arca](/arca) |

Cloud, Automagik's hosted service, is still in design and is not live.
