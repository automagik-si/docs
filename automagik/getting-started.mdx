## After Desktop opens

1. **Your companion**. Create and name your **Main Bot**.
2. **Grow your team**. Omni starts asleep; use its power button if you want it on your team.
   You can add **Genie** here too.
3. **Connect Omni** is only needed if you woke Omni. If you did not connect it during the
   team step, enter your Omni's address and choose **Get a code**. Scan the QR code and send
   the prefilled message from your phone, or use phone approval if your Omni offers it.
4. **Connect AI**. Choose an account you already use or enter an API key for your provider.

Then start a conversation with your **Main Bot**.

Need to set up your own messaging service? Start with the [Omni quickstart](/omni/quickstart).
For coding agents, continue with [Herdr and the Automagik pack](/automagik/install/herdr).
