## After Desktop opens

1. Follow the octopus welcome. You can skip either connection step and return later.
2. **Connect Omni** if you want your chat channels in the app. Enter the address your team
   gave you and choose **Get a code**. Scan the QR code and send the prefilled message from
   your phone, or use the phone approval option if your Omni offers it.
3. **Connect AI**. Choose an account you already use or enter an API key for your provider.
4. Start a conversation with your **Main Bot**.

Need to set up your own messaging service? Start with the [Omni quickstart](/omni/quickstart).
For coding agents, continue with [Herdr and the Automagik pack](/automagik/install/herdr).
