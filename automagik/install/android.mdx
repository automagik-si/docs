title: "Mobile on Android"
description: "Install Automagik Pocket and pair your Android phone with Herdr."
---

Automagik Pocket puts your coding agents on your phone. You need **Android 8.0 or newer**
and a computer running Herdr with **Pocket Link**. The Desktop app alone does not provide
the phone connection.

## Install the app

The download below is the APK named by Pocket's update feed, `pocket-lane/latest.json`;
Pocket releases move independently of Desktop.

1. On your phone, [download the Android APK][apk].
2. If you still have the old **Herdr Pocket** app, uninstall it first. It cannot update
   into Automagik, and you will need to pair again.
3. Open the downloaded APK. When Android asks, allow the browser or file manager you used
   to install apps, then confirm **Install**.
4. Open **Automagik**.

## Set up the computer

Use a Mac or Linux computer with Herdr, Node.js 20.3 or newer, and herdr-core installed.
[The Herdr guide](/automagik/install/herdr) sets up Herdr and the Automagik pack.
**Pocket Link is separate from the pack** and must also be installed.

Ask your coding agent on that computer to add Pocket Link from the
[Automagik source archive][source]: copy `herdr/mods/pocket-link` into Herdr's mod folder
and merge its `keys.json` into Herdr's key configuration. Pocket Link is maintained from source;
the pack installer does not install or update it.

1. In Herdr, press **⌘⌥P** on Mac or **Ctrl+Alt+P** on Linux to open Pocket Link.
2. On first use, choose **Install the bridge**, then **Turn on**.
3. Choose **Pair a phone**. Leave the QR dialog open while you pair.

## Pair your phone

1. In Automagik on Android, tap **Pair with Herdr desktop**, then **Scan the QR code**.
   You can paste the pairing link instead.
2. Scan the computer's QR code. The code works once, for ten minutes, and expires when
   you close the dialog.
3. Confirm your computer appears in the app and open one of its agents or terminals.

## Updates and troubleshooting

Use **Settings → About → Check for updates** on the phone. Updating closes its connections;
open the app again to reconnect. Depending on your Android version and install permissions,
an update may install automatically or ask you to confirm.

- **The QR code expired:** choose **Pair a phone** again and scan the new code.
- **No computer connection:** check that Pocket Link is turned on and its bridge installed.
  The phone does not connect by SSH.
- **Android refuses an update:** a debug build or a copy signed with another key cannot
  update to the release. Uninstall that copy, install the release, and pair again.
- **The phone lost its connection after restarting:** open the app and connect again.
- **The phone asks you to update Pocket Link:** update the computer's mod too. Updating
  the phone or Automagik pack does not update Pocket Link.
