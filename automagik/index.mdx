Automagik puts your chats and a team of AI helpers in one window. Each **Bot** has its own
name, role, model and memory. Bots talk to you and each other, work in group chats, run
scheduled tasks, and use a browser of their own.

## Two ways to work

**Team mode** is the Desktop app. Connect an [Omni](/omni) to bring your WhatsApp,
Telegram, Slack and other channels into the same window. Connect an AI account to give
your Bots a model. The team runs on DeepSeek Harness (DSH).

**Code mode** puts your coding agents in the Herdr window with the Automagik pack:
spaces, Bots and workers across coding harnesses. The Desktop app's mode switch brings
Herdr forward for your current Bot's folder. Code mode needs **both Herdr and the pack**;
downloading Desktop alone does not set it up.

Already use DSH? Install the Bot team into your existing DSH through its plugin installer.
